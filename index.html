Sage Fantasy GM

Personal fantasy football analysis and decision support.

About

Sage Fantasy GM is a private, non-commercial fantasy football analysis application designed for personal household use.

The application uses authorized, read-only Yahoo Fantasy Sports data to help analyze fantasy football teams and provide decision-support information to their managers.

Yahoo Fantasy Sports Integration

Sage Fantasy GM uses Yahoo Fantasy Sports data to analyze:

  • Fantasy football rosters
  • League settings and scoring rules
  • League standings and matchups
  • Available fantasy players
  • Roster strengths and weaknesses

This information is used to generate personalized lineup, waiver-wire, trade, matchup, and roster-management recommendations.

Intended Users

Sage Fantasy GM is currently intended for two users within a single household managing their personal Yahoo Fantasy Football teams.

Data Use

Yahoo Fantasy Sports data accessed by Sage Fantasy GM is used solely for private fantasy football analysis. The application does not sell, redistribute, or publicly display Yahoo Fantasy Sports data.

Access to Yahoo Fantasy Sports is read-only. Sage Fantasy GM does not perform roster transactions or modify Yahoo Fantasy Sports accounts.
